Co-op Fintech for member accounts and loans

Bring member records, loan applications and recovery reports into the daily work of your cooperative society.

Who it is for

Staff and administrators at cooperative credit societies and member-owned lenders. You handle member enquiries, prepare loan applications and track repayments.

The problem

A member asks about a balance or a loan application. You need to find the account history, check the repayment position and see what is still pending. Preparing the recovery sheet means bringing those records together again.

What the demo shows

Look up a member's account

Search the sample member list and open a profile. Review the financial summary, including savings, shares, deposits and loans. Open the passbook to inspect recorded transactions.

Prepare a loan application

Select a member and check eligibility. Enter the loan amount and term, preview the monthly instalment and select guarantors. Review the completed application.

Review recovery and loan reports

Open the recovery sheet to inspect amounts due from sample members. Review the loan report or retrieve a member statement. Download a report for further review.

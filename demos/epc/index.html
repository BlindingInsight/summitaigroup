Engineering calculations and piping estimates

Work through inputs, assumptions and results for process plant engineering, procurement and construction projects.

Who it is for

Estimators building piping scopes and process engineers checking equipment duties. You need to review how an estimate or sizing result follows from the inputs.

The problem

An estimate depends on more than quantities. Access conditions, labour assumptions and additional work change the total. Engineering calculations bring their own inputs and constraints. Reviewing the answer means tracking down the assumptions behind it.

What the demo shows

Build a piping estimate

Create an estimate and add piping work by operation, size, material and quantity. Set the work difficulty and include additional costs such as scaffolding or testing. Review the labour hours and cost breakdown.

Work through pump sizing

Enter fluid properties, flow and suction and discharge conditions. Calculate the required head and hydraulic power. Add a pump curve to inspect the calculated operating point.

Review a heat exchanger calculation

Enter hot and cold stream conditions and exchanger design inputs. Review the heat duty, required area and overdesign margin. Change an input and recalculate to see its effect.

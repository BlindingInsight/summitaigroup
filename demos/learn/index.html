Personalised learning and practice

Lessons, practice feedback and progress in one place, so students and teachers can see what to work on next.

Who it is for

Students studying school science and mathematics or preparing for entrance exams, and teachers deciding where each learner needs help.

The problem

A test score tells you how a student performed. It does not tell you which earlier concept needs another look. Students need a useful next task. Teachers need to see the gaps behind the marks before assigning more work.

What the demo shows

Follow a learner's next task

Choose a sample learner and open their dashboard. See lessons in progress, assigned work and suggested next steps. Open a lesson to read its content and explore the concepts it covers.

Try practice and feedback

Submit an answer and review the feedback, including an explanation when the answer is wrong. Then inspect the learner's concept progress.

Review progress as a teacher

Open the teacher view to compare learners by subject and course. Inspect an individual learner's strengths, gaps and recent activity. Assign a lesson for that learner to work on next.

Ask the tutor a question

Ask the tutor a question about the lesson and view a worked hint alongside the lesson content.

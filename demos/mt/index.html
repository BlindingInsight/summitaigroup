Manufacturing Twin for production decisions

See what is happening on the shop floor and compare production schedules with the whole factory in view.

Who it is for

Plant managers, production planners and owners of make-to-order or engineer-to-order factories. You need to decide what runs next when jobs compete for the same equipment.

The problem

The floor, the planning spreadsheet and the order book tell different stories. Work queues build up between stages. A priority changes, and you need to know what that means for the rest of the schedule.

What the demo shows

See work across the shop floor

Open the production board to see jobs across production stages. Review work in progress and stage status in the sample factory.

Compare production schedules

Open the Scheduler Operations screen to review the current plan and generate an optimised plan. Compare the plans side by side for scheduled jobs and projected work in progress. Review equipment utilisation for the optimised plan.

Check the impact of a rush order

Enter a sample order and its requested due date. Review the estimated effect on other jobs, overtime and costs. Compare alternative due dates before deciding what to promise.

Find recurring bottlenecks

Explore the bottleneck history by production stage and time period. See where constraints recur and inspect the events behind the summary.

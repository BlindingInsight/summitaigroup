PharmaReg for regulatory document review

Review draft text alongside its supporting evidence and see where each document stands in the review process.

Who it is for

Regulatory writers, scientific reviewers and regulatory affairs leads at biopharma companies. You prepare submission documents and need to check the evidence behind each claim.

The problem

A draft brings together findings from multiple reports. Reviewers need to find the passage behind a statement, resolve comments and keep track of the version under review. The work slows when the text, evidence and review status sit apart.

What the demo shows

Open a project and its documents

Explore a sample regulatory project. Review its document list and see which drafts are in progress, under review or signed off internally. Open a document from the project view.

Check a draft against its sources

Read a sample nonclinical overview by section. Follow a citation to its source passage and compare the draft statement with the supporting text.

Generating new draft text requires your own Anthropic or OpenAI key, which you enter in the demo. Reading, citations, comments and the audit log do not.

Inspect review work and history

Inspect comments attached to the draft and see the work assigned to different review roles. Open the audit log to follow recorded review actions and document status changes.
